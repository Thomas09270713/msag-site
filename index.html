01Why asset management

Well run is not the same as well invested

Your property manager is paid to operate the building: leasing, maintenance, collections and staff. No one on that team is responsible for whether the property is meeting your investment goals. That's the asset manager's job, and it's the only thing MSAG does.

Two different jobs

Property manager and asset manager

You need both. They answer different questions, and they shouldn't be the same company.

  1. Week 1Intro callThirty minutes on the property, the hold and what you need. No preparation required.
